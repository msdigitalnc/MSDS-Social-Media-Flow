Is your website hacked?

The signs are often quieter than you'd expect. Swipe for what to check.

Sign 1

A page that loads slowly

Slower pages can be a warning sign, not just a hosting issue. Compare load times with what you saw last month.

Sign 2

A user account you don't recognize

Check your user list for administrator accounts you didn't create.

Sign 3

A visitor mentions something strange

If a customer reports odd pop-ups, redirects, or content, take it seriously and check your site right away.
