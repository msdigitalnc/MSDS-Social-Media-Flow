Sales & Marketing

SalespersonTurns visitor interest into action by presenting your value and guiding people toward contacting you.
Product SpecialistExplains what services do and why they matter, focusing on outcomes rather than feature lists.
Marketing AssistantCaptures leads and nurtures them with automated follow-up, such as email sequences and retargeting.
Public Relations AssistantDisplays reputation signals like testimonials, case studies, and reviews to build trust.
Research AssistantUses SEO and analytics data to learn what customers search for and how they behave on the site.
